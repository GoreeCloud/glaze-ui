# Contributing to GLAZE UI

GLAZE UI V1.5 (`1.5.0`) is GoreeCloud's current Official, Stable, consumer-eligible shared visual and interaction design system. V1.4.1 is the immediately preceding known-good Stable rollback baseline, while V1.4.0 and earlier Stable releases remain preserved historical provenance.

Current Stable work must preserve accessibility, semantic clarity, platform-native behavior, exact-revision evidence, consumer-governance boundaries, presentation-only authority, and GoreeCloud repository/revision-control requirements. Changes to the V1.5 line must preserve the accepted V1.5.0 Context + Capability Resolution contract and its fail-closed authority boundaries unless a separately governed later release explicitly supersedes them.

Do not change `VERSION`, `registry/lifecycle.json`, official Candidate/Stable entrypoints, consumer eligibility, or downstream conformance as a side effect of ordinary implementation or qualification work. Lifecycle promotion requires a separate governed decision with fresh exact-revision evidence. Human, representative-runtime, physical-device, accessibility, and performance gates may be supported by automation, but automation must not impersonate the required review or acceptance authority.

GLAZE UI V1.5.1 / `1.5.1-dev.1` is Development follow-up work, not a Release Candidate or Stable release. Its repository-local qualification tooling may collect and validate evidence structure, but the two remaining external obligations—representative performance qualification and representative platform/posture continuity—remain pending until real evidence is independently reviewed and durably accepted.

New behavior that is not already part of the V1.5.0 Stable contract must remain in an explicitly governed Candidate/follow-up namespace until formal promotion and must be accompanied by applicable contracts, tests, rendered/native evidence, accessibility coverage, migration/rollback considerations, and documentation. Downstream applications remain independently responsible for repository-local conformance and production acceptance; Glaze Stable status does not transfer consumer acceptance.

Git history remains the required revision audit trail. Historical release and Candidate records may retain their original version language when they are explicitly historical or provenance-only; current front-door guidance must always derive lifecycle truth from `VERSION` and `registry/lifecycle.json`. At this revision, current Stable authority is GLAZE UI V1.5 / `1.5.0`, with `1.4.1` retained as the immediate rollback baseline.
