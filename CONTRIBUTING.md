# Contributing to GLAZE UI

GLAZE UI V1.4 (`1.4.1`) is GoreeCloud's current Official, Stable, consumer-eligible shared visual and interaction design system. V1.4.0 remains the immediately preceding known-good Stable rollback baseline and its acceptance evidence is preserved without reinterpretation.

Current Stable work must preserve accessibility, semantic clarity, platform-native behavior, exact-revision evidence, consumer-governance boundaries, and GoreeCloud repository/revision-control requirements. Changes to the V1.4 line must additionally preserve the V1.4.1 Stable contract, its accepted human/manual/physical qualification boundary, and V1.4.0 rollback authority unless a separately governed later release supersedes them.

Do not change `VERSION`, `registry/lifecycle.json`, official Candidate/Stable entrypoints, consumer eligibility, or downstream conformance as a side effect of ordinary implementation or qualification work. Lifecycle promotion requires a separate governed decision with fresh exact-revision evidence. Human and physical-device gates may be supported by automation, but automation must not impersonate the required review authority.

New behavior that is not already part of the V1.4.1 Stable contract must remain in an explicitly governed Candidate/follow-up namespace until formal promotion and must be accompanied by applicable contracts, tests, rendered/native evidence, accessibility coverage, migration/rollback considerations, and documentation. Downstream applications remain independently responsible for repository-local conformance and production acceptance.

Git history remains the required revision audit trail. Historical release and Candidate records may retain their original version language when they are explicitly historical or provenance-only; current front-door guidance must always derive lifecycle truth from `VERSION` and `registry/lifecycle.json`. At this revision, current Stable authority is GLAZE UI V1.4 / `1.4.1`, with `1.4.0` retained as the immediate rollback baseline.
