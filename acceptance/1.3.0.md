## Material boundaries preserved

- Solid and Raised remain the default ordinary content surfaces.
- Functional Glass is intended primarily for navigation, controls, toolbars, floating actions, and transient chrome.
- Clear Glass is reserved for controls over visually rich media.
- Glass effects are not a universal card treatment.
- Reduced-transparency, forced-colors, no-backdrop-filter, and reduced-motion fallbacks remain mandatory.
