## Authority boundaries

Glaze UI governs presentation and interaction feedback. Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and application logic remain authoritative for their underlying states. Presentation cannot manufacture privacy, security, resilience, coordination, availability, selection, busy, validation, completion, or workflow truth.

## Consumer effect

Promotion makes `1.5.0` the mandatory current Stable target. Consumers still on older releases become `migration-required`; design-system promotion does not certify downstream application readiness.

## Rollback

Before merge, rollback is branch/PR-level. After merge, rollback uses a documented revert or successor patch while preserving the 1.4.0 and 1.5.0 release evidence for audit and controlled migration.
