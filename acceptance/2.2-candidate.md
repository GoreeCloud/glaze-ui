## 2. Optical Reachability presentation boundary

The active 2.2 visual direction is **Optical Reachability**: an original GoreeCloud synthesis of spacious, reachable, modular interaction geometry and selective optical Glaze depth. It is not a clone of another platform.

The bounded presentation contract enforces these rules:

- solid where users primarily read, inspect data, or make explicit critical decisions;
- Glazed where users interact with transient navigation, command, search, or feedback chrome;
- sculpted controls and contained selected states rather than decorative edge-only selection;
- 48 px touch-oriented target floors and 56 px Touch Assistance / far-view floors;
- Deep Dark hierarchy without large high-luminance surfaces;
- Reduced Transparency removes backdrop effects while preserving hierarchy;
- Forced Colors removes decorative Glaze and preserves system colors, focus, and semantics;
- 200% text, RTL, keyboard, touch, and pointer paths remain first-class;
- Critical System remains solid and explicit;
- accessibility and semantic state always outrank optical treatment.

The component presentation is validated separately from the lower-tier component contracts so a visual refinement cannot silently weaken component semantics or acceptance.
