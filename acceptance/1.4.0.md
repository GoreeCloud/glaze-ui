## Scope

1.4 promotes Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Wide Desktop, and TV form-factor semantics while retaining the complete 1.3 expressive, material, accessibility, privacy, and resilience foundation. TV remains a far-viewing, directional-focus environment rather than a Wide Desktop layout.

## Native applicability assessment

The Glaze UI repository ships platform-neutral semantics, CSS/reference implementations, and validation—not an Android, iOS/iPadOS/tvOS, native Linux, or television-hardware runtime. Native/real-device execution is therefore not an applicable design-system-core gate for 1.4. Consumer applications must still complete their own native mapping and representative device acceptance before claiming application-level 1.4 conformance or production readiness.

## Compatibility and migration

1.4 is a compatible minor release. Glaze UI 1.3.0 remains a supported older Stable target. Existing consumers are not migrated automatically: Manager remains on 1.3, the public website consumer remains on 1.1, Tasks remains an Adoption Candidate against 1.3, and unverified consumers remain unverified until separately audited.

## Rollback

Before merge, rollback is branch closure/reset to Stable main. After the guarded promotion merge, the resulting Stable merge commit is the design-system rollback point. Downstream consumers remain independently versioned and do not require automatic rollback or migration.
