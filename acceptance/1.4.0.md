## Scope

1.4 promotes Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Wide Desktop, and TV form-factor semantics while retaining the complete 1.3 expressive, material, accessibility, privacy, and resilience foundation. TV remains a far-viewing, directional-focus environment rather than a Wide Desktop layout.

## Native applicability assessment

The Glaze UI repository ships platform-neutral semantics, CSS/reference implementations, and validation—not an Android, iOS/iPadOS/tvOS, native Linux, or television-hardware runtime. Native/real-device execution was therefore not an applicable design-system-core gate for 1.4. Consumer applications must still complete their own native mapping and representative device acceptance before claiming application-level 1.4 conformance or production readiness.

## Compatibility and migration

1.4 is a compatible minor release. Existing consumers were not migrated automatically. Manager remains on 1.3.0, the public website consumer remains on 1.1.0, Tasks remains an Adoption Candidate against 1.3.0 pending its own final application acceptance, and unverified consumers remain unverified until separately audited.

## Rollback

The Stable design-system rollback point is merge commit `01c86323f8b747373d308026adc8b0881855cdc5`. If a material Glaze UI 1.4 regression is discovered, revert that commit through normal source-control review. Downstream consumers remain independently versioned and do not require automatic rollback or migration.
