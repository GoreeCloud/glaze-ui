## Acceptance policy

GLAZE UI V1.4.0 is accepted as an automated/code-complete Stable release when the exact promotion revision passes the V1.4 Stable-authority workflow and the inherited V1.4 automated qualification workflows applicable to the changed release surface.

The project owner explicitly directed that human validation and human verification move to V1.4.1 rather than block V1.4.0. Those items remain deferred/unverified; this Stable record does not convert them into passing evidence.

Stable authority consists of:

- `VERSION`

## Deferred V1.4.1 evidence

Human optical review, manual keyboard/focus traversal, human-operated assistive-technology qualification, subjective quality review, representative human workflow validation, physical-device/OEM/compositor qualification, native-platform observation, physical-device production-performance/power observation, and consumer-specific human sign-off are deferred to V1.4.1.

None are represented as passed by V1.4.0.

## Consumer boundary

No GoreeCloud application or service automatically becomes V1.4-conformant or production-ready because Glaze UI V1.4.0 is Stable. Consumers must explicitly migrate and retain repository-local evidence for their applicable platform, accessibility, performance, workflow, rollback, and release acceptance boundaries.

## Rollback

GLAZE UI V1.3.0 remains the immediately preceding Stable rollback target. A material V1.4 regression should be corrected through normal current-line source control; published evidence must not be silently rewritten.
