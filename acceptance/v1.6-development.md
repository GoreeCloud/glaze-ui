## Authority boundary

Evidence must be bound to the exact reviewed V1.6 revision. Missing evidence, stale evidence, revision mismatch, unsupported evidence type, or an unjustified not-applicable disposition remains open and must not be inferred passing.

## Core pillars

1. Skeleton Motion
2. Perceived Performance
3. State Clarity
4. Accessibility Expansion
5. Motion Coherence
6. Material Intelligence
7. Interaction Continuity
8. Design-System Enforcement

## Governing principle

Waiting, changing, adapting, loading, failing, recovering, and transitioning must be as intentionally designed as the final static interface.

V1.6 should remain alive without being distracting, adaptive without being unpredictable, beautiful without sacrificing clarity, and sophisticated without sacrificing accessibility or performance.

## Current qualification disposition

The matrix definition itself is Development source. No V1.6 qualification lane is accepted by this document merely because the lane exists. Actual evidence must be collected, reviewed, and recorded against the exact governed qualification revision before any lifecycle review may treat the lane as satisfied.
