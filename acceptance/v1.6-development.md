**V1.6 Development aggregate target:** `1.6.0-dev.12`  
**Consumer eligible:** No

## Authority boundary

This record defines the V1.6 qualification lanes from planned specification section 98 and records the final section 99/100 pillars and governing principle. It does not establish Candidate, Release Candidate, Stable, downstream consumer, deployment, or production acceptance.

Evidence must be bound to the exact reviewed V1.6 revision. Missing evidence, stale evidence, revision mismatch, unsupported evidence type, an unsatisfied required evidence group, or an unjustified not-applicable disposition remains open and must not be inferred passing.

Where a lane lists more than one required evidence group, **every group must be satisfied on the same exact reviewed revision**. Alternatives inside one group are interchangeable only within that group. For example, Accessibility requires machine evidence **and** either human or assistive-technology evidence; one machine record alone is not enough.

## Evidence-group semantics

Each applicable lane is modeled as one or more required evidence groups:

- Every required group must be satisfied.
- One evidence record may satisfy only groups whose allowed evidence types include that record.
- All accepted records must target the same exact reviewed V1.6 revision.
- A record must be explicitly verified and carry a durable evidence reference.
- Evidence from a different revision, an unsupported evidence type, a missing reference, or a partially satisfied group does not count.
- A not-applicable disposition requires an explicit justification and does not silently inherit from another product, platform, or revision.

The grouped model intentionally prevents machine checks from substituting for required human, assistive-technology, rendered, device, or performance evidence.

## Governing principle

Waiting, changing, adapting, loading, failing, recovering, and transitioning must be as intentionally designed as the final static interface.

V1.6 should remain alive without being distracting, adaptive without being unpredictable, beautiful without sacrificing clarity, and sophisticated without sacrificing accessibility or performance.

## Current qualification disposition

The matrix definition itself is Development source. No V1.6 qualification lane is accepted by this document merely because the lane exists. Actual evidence must be collected, reviewed, and recorded against one exact governed qualification revision before any lifecycle review may treat the lane as satisfied.

The V1.6 Development source is represented through sections 1–100, but source completeness is not qualification closure. The evidence-group hardening in `1.6.0-dev.12` exists specifically to prevent partial evidence from being mistaken for completed qualification.
