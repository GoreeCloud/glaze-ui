# GLAZE UI V1.2 — Android Native Frosted Neutral Candidate Evidence

## Purpose

This acceptance track proves that the GLAZE UI V1.2 Frosted Neutral Candidate has a framework-native Android handheld reference with bounded emulator evidence. It carries the neutral frosted material direction onto Android while preserving GLAZE UI V1.1 / 1.1.0 as current Stable authority.

## Candidate material contract

The Android reference follows the V1.2 material rule: neutral glass first, color second.

- Base, Raised, Overlay, and Panel substrates are neutral-channel materials in Light, Dark, and Deep Dark.
- Light uses milky white translucent material over a neutral wallpaper field.
- Dark and Deep Dark use smoky neutral graphite glass rather than blue-green or teal substrate tint.
- GoreeCloud blue and semantic colors are reserved for active controls, focus, status, progress, and identity.
- Reduced Transparency removes translucent material dependence and resolves to opaque neutral surfaces.
- Critical System remains high-opacity and non-backdrop-dependent.
- Nested controls inside the dominant Control Center do not become independent blur authorities.

## Native reference coverage

The framework-native Android reference includes:

- Frosted Neutral Candidate header and material contract summary.
- Candidate lifecycle and appearance evidence.
- Universal Search neutral material example.
- Quick Settings / Control Center with neutral tiles and accent-only active states.
- Critical System high-opacity example.
- Native primary and secondary actions.
- Light, Dark, and Deep Dark appearances.
- Reduced Transparency.
- Touch Assistance with a 56 dp minimum target.
- 200% text-scale reachability in the Deep Dark + Touch Assistance case.

## Automated emulator gate

`scripts/validate_glaze_v1_2_android_runtime.py` runs against a freshly booted Android emulator and fails closed when the expected Candidate contract is not present.

The gate verifies:

1. Exact source revision equality with the workflow revision.
2. Neutral-channel Base, Raised, Overlay, Panel, and Critical System source assignments.
3. Candidate lifecycle and material-contract labels.
4. Light / neutral translucent material rendering.
5. Dark / Reduced Transparency opaque fallback.
6. Deep Dark / 200% text / Touch Assistance reachability.
7. 48 dp minimum action targets.
8. 56 dp Touch Assistance minimum action targets.
9. Quick Settings tile target geometry.
10. Basic native interaction state change.
11. PNG screenshot generation and SHA-256 evidence hashes.
12. Android SDK, build fingerprint, density, and source revision evidence capture.

## Evidence outputs

A passing workflow publishes:

- the debug Candidate APK;
- Light Frosted Neutral screenshot evidence;
- Dark Reduced Transparency screenshot evidence;
- Deep Dark + 200% text + Touch Assistance screenshot evidence;
- `android-native-evidence.json` containing revision, platform, target measurements, screenshot hashes, and explicit evidence boundaries.

## Boundaries

Passing this track is **Candidate evidence only**. It does not establish:

- V1.2 Stable promotion;
- human optical approval across Android OEM skins;
- OEM-wide or hardware-compositor blur fidelity;
- physical-device qualification;
- TalkBack certification;
- app signing or distribution acceptance;
- downstream GoreeCloud application conformance;
- production deployment eligibility.

Those remain separate release and adoption gates.
