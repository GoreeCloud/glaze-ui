# GLAZE UI V1.4.1 Stable Acceptance

**Decision date:** 2026-09-14  
**Decision:** Accepted as Official Stable, subject to the exact-head release and promotion gates in this repository.  
**Consumer eligible:** Yes  
**Stable baseline:** `1.4.0`  
**Qualified implementation anchor:** `66478aed461b83c49b2ed027c3e4afc26520e98c`

## Accepted technical scope

- The complete V1.4.0 Stable foundation remains preserved as the immediate rollback baseline.
- The V1.4.1 Stable CSS blob is byte-identical to the human-reviewed Candidate CSS blob.
- The V1.4.1 Stable optical-engine blob is byte-identical to the human-reviewed Candidate optical-engine blob.
- The Stable runtime entrypoint inherits V1.4.0 and exports the promoted V1.4.1 engine.
- Adapter faults fail safe without granting telemetry or remote-reporting authority.
- Capability-aware optical performance scaling is local, bounded, downgrade-only in session, and subordinate to accessibility authority.
- Semantic protection strengthens as decorative optical work is reduced.
- V1.4.0 source and historical evidence are not rewritten by this patch promotion.

## Human-validation acceptance

The governed V1.4.1 human-validation matrix is accepted for the supported shared design-system claim. The canonical fail-closed promotion verifier must pass against the exact release-head revision before merge.

Historical positive results remain bound to their actual tested revisions. Where a result predates the release head, promotion uses an explicit `unaffected` continuity assessment rather than relabeling the historical evidence as a newer-SHA execution.

The project owner explicitly bound the formerly provenance-ambiguous PASS outcomes for switch, voice, supported assistive technologies, Android/OEM, Linux compositor/window, desktop, tablet, TV, foldable, and thermal/power to exact reviewed revision `8a856b3aa3649c2802b65702744ec9634768b14d`. Missing historical reviewer/device/runtime/timestamp fields remain provenance gaps and are not fabricated.

Wearable/watch is not applicable to the shared V1.4.1 production claim and remains separately governed as non-production wearable work.

## Consumer boundary

Promotion of the shared design system to `1.4.1` does not automatically make any GoreeCloud application or website conformant, Stable, or production-eligible. The central consumer target advances to `1.4.1`, and each consumer must complete repository-local adoption and exact-revision acceptance for its supported platforms.

## Completion boundary

This acceptance record authorizes Stable promotion only when all of the following are simultaneously true on the final release head:

- `VERSION` and `registry/lifecycle.json` identify `1.4.1` as current Official Stable and consumer-eligible;
- the V1.4.1 Stable source verifier passes;
- the V1.4.0 Stable-preservation verifier passes;
- the lifecycle/documentation/consumer consistency gates pass;
- the canonical V1.4.1 human-validation promotion gate passes for the exact release head; and
- the governed release PR is merged into `main` without source drift.
