- fail-closed source and rendered validation integrated into exact-revision CI.

## Accepted Candidate evidence

The Candidate evidence is implemented by the 2.0 reference surfaces and validators under `reference/` and `scripts/`, including the core matrix, resilience/foldable cases, isolated increased-contrast emulation, and wearable/spatial environment gate.

Exact-revision CI run `33160168954` passed the full retained Stable suites and every currently implemented Glaze UI 2.0 Candidate gate at revision `63d28c060f723453b0288fddd3d73c57926cac21`.

The accepted core and resilience matrix establishes:

- Light and Dark across Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, Wide Desktop, and TV;
- Deep Dark on representative mobile and desktop surfaces;
- Clear, Balanced, and Solid material clarity;
- Calm, Balanced, and Expressive expression states;
- solid content planes with Glaze interaction surfaces;
- representative Connected Transformation continuity and state-preserving fallback when View Transitions are unavailable;
- Navigation Capsule reachability, selected-state expansion, and compression without shrinking the 48px touch floor;
- Tablet rail, Desktop/Wide Desktop persistent navigation, and TV far-view navigation adaptation;
- large-text reflow, reduced-motion, reduced-transparency, forced-colors, no-`backdrop-filter`, and increased-contrast behavior;
- a representative 1114×834 book-posture foldable layout with a 24px hinge exclusion region and 280px pane floor;
- isolated `prefers-contrast: more` behavior through DevTools media emulation, independent from forced-colors.

The wearable/spatial gate additionally establishes at the design-system reference level:

- a 360×360 circular wearable composition with compact rotational navigation;
- one selected item and one roving-tabindex target at a time;
- wheel and directional-key navigation parity;
- 48px minimum rendered wearable targets in normal and reduced-motion modes;
- representative spatial Environmental Panel, Floating Control Surface, and Control Volume surfaces with semantic focusability;
- depth as supplemental presentation rather than semantic or task authority;
- a fully flattened spatial fallback with no Z-depth dependency;
- a 56px nominal spatial control floor so perspective at supported negative depth cannot reduce the rendered hit region below the 48px general minimum.

The rendered gates found and caused correction of two reachability defects rather than weakening acceptance: Navigation Capsule whole-surface scaling previously reduced 48px targets to about 45px, and negative spatial depth initially reduced a nominal 48px control to about 46.6px. Both defects are corrected while the effective 48px assertion remains unchanged.

Wearable/spatial browser reference acceptance is **not native-device certification**. Native or real-device GoreeCloud consumers must complete their own platform mapping, input, accessibility, performance, safe-area, interruption, and product acceptance before production approval.

## Dependency, privacy, and security boundary

The Candidate runtime is self-contained and dependency-free. Source validators fail closed on browser network and storage APIs, common telemetry SDK markers, unsafe dynamic-code/HTML-write primitives, remote HTTP(S)/CDN/import dependencies, and drift away from repository-local Candidate assets.

These checks establish the design-system Candidate's dependency/privacy/security boundary; they do not replace application-specific security or privacy acceptance for downstream consumers.

## Promotion readiness boundary

The substantive 2.0 Candidate implementation gates defined by this record are now evidenced. Stable promotion has **not** occurred and remains blocked on release-state work rather than another feature declaration:

- freeze and pass exact-revision CI after the acceptance/governance records are reconciled;
- perform a controlled 2.0 release-state migration of `VERSION`, Stable token/status artifacts, release validation, compatibility/migration documentation, and consumer-required-target records;
- prove that the resulting final promotion revision passes the complete Stable and 2.0 validation suite;
- merge/promote through the normal controlled release path and only then update production/public Stable claims.

The existing release-state validator is intentionally 1.x-specific and must be migrated rather than bypassed. No Candidate file may be relabeled Stable merely to satisfy version text.

## Authority boundary

Glaze UI 2.0 governs presentation and interaction. Its intelligence, Live Surface, security, privacy, resilience, and Mesh presentation patterns do not create underlying execution authority, evidence, or domain truth. Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and application logic remain authoritative for their respective substantive state.

## Consumer boundary

The 2.0 design contract is the enforced active-development migration target. Glaze UI 1.6.0 remains the last validated Stable implementation baseline until formal 2.0 promotion. A consumer adopting 2.0 Candidate behavior is not production-eligible on that basis and must complete normal application-specific acceptance after the design-system release is promoted.
