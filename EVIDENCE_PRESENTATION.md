# GLAZE UI V1.0 — Evidence Presentation

Evidence shown through GLAZE UI must preserve the authority, freshness, source, confidence, and failure state of the underlying system. Presentation may organize or summarize evidence but may not strengthen unverified claims.

V1 evidence surfaces should expose provenance when material, distinguish unavailable from negative results, avoid decorative certainty, support keyboard/touch/assistive input, and remain legible under Reduced Transparency, Increased Contrast, Forced Colors, and large text.

Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Identity, and GoreeCloud Mesh remain authoritative for their own truth domains.
