# GLAZE UI V1.0 — Icon Construction

GLAZE UI V1.0 icon construction follows a consistent optical grid, balanced stroke/shape weight, restrained detail, platform-safe clear space, and legibility at small sizes. Platform adaptations may alter geometry where native conventions require it, but semantic identity must remain recognizable.

Official identity artwork is kept under `assets/identity/official`. Experimental or rejected identity candidate sets are not part of the active V1 source tree.
