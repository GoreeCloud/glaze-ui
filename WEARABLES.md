# GLAZE UI V1.0 — Wearables

Wearable mappings prioritize glanceability, minimal interaction depth, large reachable targets, strong focus/selection state, low visual clutter, and native platform conventions. Glaze effects are secondary to battery, performance, legibility, and outdoor/variable-light use.

WatchOS, Wear OS, and other wearable implementations are not considered V1 production-accepted until exact-revision native evidence is regenerated and approved.
