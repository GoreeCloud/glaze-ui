Accessibility profiles

Set data-glaze-accessibility on an application root to combine explicit profiles such as enhanced-focus, large-targets, high-contrast, reduced-transparency, reduced-motion, and monochrome. Platform accessibility preferences remain authoritative and may not be weakened.

Authority boundary: these surfaces present supplied state only. Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and application logic remain authoritative for the truth being presented.
