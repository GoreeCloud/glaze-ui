Required real-session observations

  1. Navigate landmarks, headings, links, form fields, and buttons with the chosen assistive technology.
  2. Confirm accessible names, roles, descriptions, values, disabled/restricted meaning, and state changes are understandable.
  3. Confirm focus order follows the visible task flow and focus changes are not stolen by background updates.
  4. Trigger the live-region update and confirm the announcement is useful, non-duplicative, and non-disruptive.
  5. Exercise Permission required, Restricted, Offline, and Error states and confirm the distinction is conveyed without relying on color.
  6. Review loading/progress semantics and recovery affordances.
  7. Review at increased zoom or large text and confirm content remains navigable and understandable.

This page intentionally has no PASS button and does not create or download evidence. Record findings separately with OS/runtime, assistive-technology name/version, input method, exact source, observations, limitations, and durable evidence reference.
