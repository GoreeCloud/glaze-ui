Motion

Effects and spatial motion are different tools

Effects motion handles color, opacity, glow, and similar feedback. Spatial motion handles geometry, layout, navigation, size, and hero transitions. Reduced-motion mode removes nonessential transformation.

No remote fonts, icons, analytics, trackers, or UI dependencies are required for this reference.
