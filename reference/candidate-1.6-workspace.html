Glaze UI 1.6 Candidate Adaptive Workspace and Navigation
Overview Components Density Accessibility

Content stays primary.

The Candidate workspace layer provides reusable title, navigation, toolbar, content, inspector, and status regions that transform for Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and TV without changing semantic order or producer-authoritative state.

Navigation

Persistent where useful, overlayed when space is constrained, and always accessible.

Toolbar

Frequent actions remain visible while lower-priority commands move to overflow.

Inspector

Contextual detail remains secondary and can collapse into a sheet on compact layouts.

Input-aware density

Pointer workflows may compress visual padding while touch and TV retain larger activation floors.

Resilient

Reduced motion, reduced transparency, forced colors, keyboard focus, and no-backdrop fallbacks are part of the contract.

Inspector

The inspector is contextual and subordinate to the primary content task.
