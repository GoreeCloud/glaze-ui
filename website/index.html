Glaze UI 1.3.0 Stable

Fluid where it helps. Expressive where it matters.

Glaze UI combines ergonomic reach, restrained material depth, expressive shape and motion, and GoreeCloud privacy, accessibility, identity, and resilience into one original cross-platform design language.

Explore 1.3View source

Calm content surfaces. Functional glass for navigation and controls. Expressive shape and motion only where they strengthen hierarchy and meaning.

Foundations

Depth with restraint

Glaze uses five intentional surface levels. Solid and Raised remain the normal content surfaces; translucency is a functional hierarchy tool rather than a universal card treatment.

CanvasAtmospheric application background
SolidHigh-readability content
RaisedElevated solid panel
GlazeSelective functional translucency
OverlayAttention-priority surface

Ergonomic

Comfortable targets, clear hierarchy, adaptive navigation, safe areas, and compact reachability support practical touch-first use.

Accessible

Visible focus, 44px minimum targets, reduced motion, reduced transparency, forced-colors resilience, semantic naming, and solid fallbacks are part of the contract.

Private

No tracking UI dependencies. Remote fonts, scripts, and icons are prohibited unless explicitly justified and documented.

1.3 Expression

A stronger personality with stricter boundaries

Expression is concentrated in high-value moments so utility surfaces stay calm and predictable.

Functional Glass

Navigation, toolbars, controls, and transient chrome can float above content without turning the content layer into glass.

Expressive shape

Compact, Standard, Expressive, Hero, and Pressed geometry communicate hierarchy, grouping, selection, and interaction.

Two motion families

Effects motion handles color and opacity. Spatial motion handles geometry, layout, navigation, and meaningful transitions.

Adoption

Stable, but never automatic churn

Glaze UI 1.3.0 is Stable. Existing consumers should adopt it deliberately through application-specific semantic mapping, testing, and visual/accessibility acceptance rather than assuming a design-system promotion automatically changes every product.
