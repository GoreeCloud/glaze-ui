1.5 Stable foundations

Polished depth with practical restraint

Canvas, Solid, Raised, Functional Glass, Clear Glass, and Overlay form the governed material hierarchy. Ordinary content defaults to stable Solid/Raised surfaces; translucency is selective and must retain accessible fallbacks.

Glaze UI is an integral GoreeCloud platform system, not a cosmetic theme or decorative brand. Its name represents implemented design tokens, components, accessibility requirements, interaction semantics, form-factor behavior, semantic color roles, adoption rules, motion contracts, and conformance evidence.

Stable 1.5 form-factor contract

Adapt the experience, not the identity.

Form factor uses window, input, viewing distance, platform behavior, posture, and task—not width alone. Mobile, Tablet, Desktop, and TV are first-class Stable 1.5 environments.

Mobile

Reachable actions, touch-safe targets, safe areas, mobile navigation.

Tablet

Rails, panes, posture/window adaptation, touch-first ergonomics.

Desktop

Resizable workspaces, pointer/keyboard precision, toolbars and multi-pane workflows.

TV

Far-view readability, overscan-safe content, directional focus, remote-first operation.

Semantic color

Color communicates meaning without becoming the authority.

Glaze UI 1.5 defines adaptive semantic roles and protected tonal families for accent, selection, focus, information, success, warning, danger, privacy, security, protected, restricted, online, offline, syncing, and unavailable states.

Important meaning must also survive through text, icons, shapes, patterns, or equivalent non-color cues. Wardveil Security, Privacy Shield, Everkeep, GoreeCloud Mesh, and application logic remain authoritative for their underlying states; visual treatment cannot create or upgrade those claims.

Conformance

Stable semantics protect beauty and usability

Identity, tokens, materials, states, accessibility, privacy, resilience, product personality, layout/density, and purpose-built Mobile/Tablet/Desktop/TV fidelity remain mandatory.

A Glaze UI label never substitutes for conformance. Every GoreeCloud application must implement the applicable current Stable Glaze UI contract and complete its own source, rendered, native, accessibility, performance, and real-device acceptance where applicable.

Smartwatch and wearable applications remain production-blocked until Glaze UI contains an applicable Stable wearable interaction contract. Missing platform coverage is not an exception.

Current evidence must remain valid according to its authoritative producer. Expired, stale, superseded, or otherwise invalid evidence cannot support a current conformant state.

Adoption

1.5 is Stable and is the production target.

Every GoreeCloud-controlled user-facing application is required to target Glaze UI 1.5.0 through controlled, evidence-backed adoption. Candidate 1.6 systems may be evaluated but do not trigger migration or production-conformance claims until promoted.
