Adaptive interaction

Transform rather than resize.

The same navigation concept becomes a phone Navigation Capsule, Tablet rail, Desktop sidebar, TV focus dock, compact wearable rotational navigation, or spatial floating surface. Foldable hinges are physical exclusion regions, not decorative gaps.

Mobile

Reachability, Glaze Action Zone, 48px floor.

Tablet

Rail/panes and posture-aware task continuity.

Desktop

Persistent tools, pointer/keyboard and resizable workspaces.

TV

Far view, 56px floor, directional focus.

Wearable

Compact rotational navigation; not a shrunken phone.

Spatial

Depth is supplemental; fully flat fallback remains operable.

Accessibility and resilience

Effects are optional. Usability is not.

Reduced transparency increases opacity. Increased contrast strengthens boundaries. Reduced motion removes nonessential travel/morphing. Large text reorganizes instead of clipping. Forced colors and no-backdrop/no-depth modes preserve state and task completion.

Current Stable target

2.0 is Stable and is the production target.

Glaze UI 1.6.0 is the immediately preceding historical Stable baseline. Existing 1.x consumers are migration-required; no downstream application becomes 2.0-conformant by declaration. Native/hardware products still complete application-specific native and real-device acceptance.
