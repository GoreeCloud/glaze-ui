Foundations

Polished depth with practical restraint

Canvas, Solid, Raised, Glaze, and Overlay form the material hierarchy. Functional Glass is selective; readability remains primary.

Glaze UI is an integral GoreeCloud platform system, not a cosmetic theme or decorative brand. Its name represents implemented design tokens, components, accessibility requirements, interaction semantics, form-factor behavior, adoption rules, and conformance evidence.

Conformance

Stable semantics protect beauty and usability

Identity, tokens, surfaces, states, accessibility, privacy, resilience, product personality, and purpose-built Mobile/Tablet/Desktop/TV fidelity remain mandatory.

A Glaze UI label never substitutes for conformance. Products must implement the applicable stable contracts and preserve their own product purpose, accessibility, security, privacy, and resilience requirements.

Adoption

1.4 is Stable; migration remains controlled.

Existing consumers may stay on supported older Stable releases. No GoreeCloud application is automatically migrated by the design-system promotion.
