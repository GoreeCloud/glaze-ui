System Shell

Workspace → Application → System Overlay → System Panel → Critical System

System priority becomes progressively more explicit and solid. Durable content stays readable; transient command, search, control and feedback chrome may use Glaze. Ordinary composition permits one dominant Glaze panel plus one to three small floating Glaze controls.

Adaptive interaction

Transform rather than resize.

The same navigation concept may become a phone Capsule, Tablet rail, Desktop sidebar, TV focus dock, compact wearable rotational navigation, or spatial floating surface. Foldable hinges are physical exclusion regions, not decorative gaps.

Mobile

Reachability, safe areas, 48px touch-oriented floor.

Tablet

Rail/panes and posture-aware task continuity.

Desktop

Persistent tools, pointer/keyboard and resizable workspaces.

TV

Far view, 56px floor where applicable, directional focus.

Wearable

Compact rotational navigation; not a shrunken phone.

Spatial

Depth is supplemental; fully flat fallback remains operable.

Accessibility and resilience

Effects are optional. Usability is not.

Reduced Transparency resolves optical material to solid hierarchy. Increased Contrast strengthens boundaries. Reduced Motion removes nonessential travel/morphing. 200% text reorganizes instead of clipping. Forced Colors, RTL/localization, Touch Assistance and no-backdrop/no-depth modes preserve state and task completion.

Current Stable target

2.2 is Stable and is the production design-system target.

Glaze UI 2.1.0 is the immediately preceding historical Stable rollback baseline. Existing 2.1 and earlier consumers are migration-required; no downstream application becomes 2.2-conformant by declaration. Native/hardware products still complete application-specific native, accessibility and representative physical-device acceptance.
