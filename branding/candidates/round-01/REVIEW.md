# Glaze UI Identity Round 01 - Review Rubric

Status: **Review protocol only. No candidate is approved or canonical.**

Technical CI may verify source safety and review boundaries, but it cannot approve aesthetics.

## Required review modes

Every candidate must be reviewed in all of these modes before approval:

1. 128 px presentation size.
2. 24 px compact application-navigation size.
3. 16 px favicon-size recognition.
4. Light background.
5. Dark background.
6. Monochrome presentation.

## Decision criteria

| Criterion | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Distinctiveness | Is the symbol recognizable by shape and concept rather than palette? |
| Glaze relationship | Does it communicate layered surfaces, hierarchy, polish, controlled translucency, expressive geometry, or motion without becoming a literal glass-pane cliche? |
| Compact recognition | Does the identity remain understandable at 24 px and 16 px? |
| Monochrome viability | Does the symbol remain distinct when gradients and color differences are removed? |
| Light/dark viability | Can the same underlying identity work on both appearance families without becoming a different mark? |
| Portfolio separation | Is it clearly different from the GoreeCloud platform logo, Browser, Privacy Shield, Wardveil Security, Everkeep, and ordinary Suite application icons? |
| Originality | Does it avoid obvious resemblance to external-vendor identity systems or the previously rejected rounded-loop G direction? |
| Longevity | Can the symbol plausibly remain useful as Glaze UI evolves without being tied to a temporary visual trend? |

## Candidate-specific questions

### 01 - Layered Aperture

- Does the aperture remain intentional at favicon size?
- Are the offset layers distinctive enough in monochrome?
- Does the silhouette feel like a design-system identity rather than a generic application tile?

### 02 - Flow Bands

- Do three bands remain independently legible at compact sizes?
- Does the symbol convey controlled motion without reading as a network, wave, or connectivity logo?
- Can monochrome treatment preserve hierarchy without relying on gradient separation?

### 03 - Faceted Surface

- Does the central structure remain clear at 16-24 px?
- Does the geometry communicate adaptive surfaces without becoming too architectural or rigid?
- Is the asymmetric silhouette memorable enough to function as a standalone identity?

## Approval boundary

Approval must name one exact candidate ID and its recorded Git blob SHA from `manifest.json`. A concept name, color direction, or approximate appearance alone is not sufficient to authorize canonical promotion.

If none is strong enough, the correct decision is **reject Round 01** and create a materially different Round 02. There is no requirement to choose a winner.
