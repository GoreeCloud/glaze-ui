# GLAZE UI V1.0 — Motion

V1 motion explains relationship, continuity, hierarchy, and direct manipulation. It must remain interruptible where user-driven, avoid blocking keyboard traversal, and respect Reduced Motion.

Connected Transformation may morph related controls or surfaces when the relationship is clear. Reduced Motion substitutes shorter fades/state changes for nonessential travel. Continuous shimmer, pulsing, drift, or decorative motion requires a functional reason and must not impair focus or reading.

Glaze Motion is separately governed as an experimental subsystem unless explicitly incorporated into a future V1.x contract.
