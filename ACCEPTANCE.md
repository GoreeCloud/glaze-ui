# GLAZE UI V1.4 Acceptance

GLAZE UI V1.4 / `1.4.0` is the current Official Stable and consumer-eligible design-system release under the explicit GoreeCloud project-owner lifecycle decision of 2026-09-13.

Stable authority includes the V1.4 contract, machine-readable lifecycle registry, Stable Web/runtime entrypoints, promoted optical-material and accessibility implementation sources, deterministic V1.4 validation and regression coverage, and the exact release acceptance boundary recorded in `acceptance/1.4.0.md`.

Human validation and human verification that require subjective judgment, manual interaction, assistive-technology operation by a human, representative physical devices, actual native-renderer observation, or physical-device performance/power observation are transferred to V1.4.1. They remain deferred/unverified and are not represented as passed V1.4.0 evidence. See `acceptance/v1.4-deferred-qualification.md` and `GLAZE_UI_V1_4_1_HARDENING.md`.

No downstream GoreeCloud application or service becomes V1.4-conformant or production-eligible automatically. Each consumer must explicitly target `1.4.0` and satisfy its own applicable acceptance boundary.
