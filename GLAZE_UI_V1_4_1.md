## Promoted behavior

V1.4.1 retains the complete V1.4.0 Stable foundation and adds the reviewed hardening layer:

- fail-safe handling for contextual signal-adapter faults;
- protected semantic surfaces for readable content and controls;
- Full, Balanced, Efficient, and Durable optical performance levels;
- bounded local capability/performance acceptance without device identity, price/model heuristics, telemetry, or remote context;
- downgrade-only in-session optical performance governance;
- stronger semantic protection as decorative optical work is reduced;
- bounded runtime evidence of requested/accepted optical richness and downgrade reasons.

Accessibility authority remains stronger than decorative performance adaptation. Forced Colors and Reduced Transparency continue to fail closed to solid accessible presentation.
